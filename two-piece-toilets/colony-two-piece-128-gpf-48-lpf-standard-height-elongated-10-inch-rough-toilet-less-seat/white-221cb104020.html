    Product Description

    The Colony Standard Height Elongated Toilet delivers the worry-free reliability that has defined the American Standard brand for more than 140 years. Colony is a low-consumption toilet that uses 20% less water than a regular toilet and is WaterSense® certified for high efficiency. The Colony toilet is easy to install and is backed by an industry-leading warranty that includes lifetime on chinaware plus 2 years on mechanical parts, twice the industry standard of 1 year. You can be confident Colony will stand the test of time.
